A compliance incident often starts as a bad paraphrase.

Banks route complaints under Regulation E and Regulation Z deadlines. A useful RAG system cannot stop at a plausible answer. It needs a source, an exact quote, and a person in the loop.

The model may read the law. It cannot rewrite it.

The agent returns one section and one quote. Pydantic rejects extra fields, and a plain substring check rejects a quote that is not word for word in the fetched section. A refusal is a valid output.

How it works

The AI chooses among retrieved evidence. Code checks the contract. A reviewer owns the final decision.

  1. Fetch public records

    Use CFPB narratives and two live eCFR section endpoints.

  2. Retrieve with BM25

    Rank the paragraphs most related to the complaint narrative.

  3. Ask for closed JSON

    Return one section, one exact quote, and a short reason.

  4. Verify and review

    Reject non-verbatim quotes, then wait for a human approval.
